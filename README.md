# 🎓 UniPak — Pakistan University Admission Portal

**UniPak** is a centralized university admission platform designed to make the admission process easier for students applying to universities across Pakistan.

Instead of searching through multiple university websites for merit lists, admission criteria, entry test details, aggregate formulas, programs, and facilities, UniPak brings all the important information together in one place.

---

## 🚩 Problem It Solves

University admission information in Pakistan is often scattered across different websites and portals.

Students usually have to separately search for:

- Admission criteria
- Aggregate formulas
- Previous closing merits
- Entry test structure
- Programs offered
- Hostel and transport facilities
- Application procedures

UniPak simplifies this process by providing these resources through one centralized and easy-to-use platform.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🏫 University Explorer
Browse universities and view important information including programs, campuses, admission criteria, facilities, and application details.

### 🧮 Aggregate Calculator
Select a university, enter your academic and entry test marks, and automatically calculate your admission aggregate using the university's specific formula.

### 📊 Admission Prediction
Compare your aggregate with previous closing merits to estimate your admission chances.

> Predictions are based on historical data and do not guarantee admission.

### ⚖️ University Comparison
Compare universities based on your aggregate, previous merit trends, and admission requirements.

### 📝 Entry Test Breakdown
View university-specific entry test information including total marks, number of questions, and subject-wise distribution.

### 🎓 Program Information
Explore undergraduate programs offered by different universities along with relevant admission information.

### 🔖 Saved Universities
Save universities you are interested in and create your own shortlist.

### 🤖 AI Admission Assistant
An AI assistant for answering common university admission-related questions.
